# Research Assistant Skill

Gather comprehensive research material to fuel compelling, credible content.

## Core Philosophy

1. **Numbers that surprise** - Statistics create hooks and credibility
2. **Stories that illustrate** - Examples make abstract concrete
3. **Angles that differentiate** - Find what others aren't saying
4. **Sources that support** - Credibility requires attribution

## Research Process

### Step 1: Define Research Scope
Before searching, clarify:
- What specific question needs answering?
- Who is the target audience?
- What depth is needed? (quick/standard/deep)
- What content format? (post, article, thread)

### Step 2: Web Research
Search for:
1. **Recent data (2024-2025)** - Recency matters for credibility
2. **Primary sources** - Studies, reports, official stats
3. **Expert opinions** - Named individuals with credentials
4. **Contrarian views** - What challenges conventional wisdom
5. **Real examples** - Companies, people, case studies

Search patterns:
- "[topic] statistics 2024 2025"
- "[topic] research study"
- "[topic] expert opinion"
- "[topic] case study example"
- "[topic] contrary unpopular opinion"
- "[topic] failed why"

### Step 3: Vault Research
Search existing knowledge:
- Related notes in Ideas/, Writings/, Projects/
- Previous content on similar topics
- Personal experiences logged in daily notes
- Connections to goals and current focus

### Step 4: Synthesis
Organize findings into:
- Key statistics (with sources)
- Expert quotes
- Real-world examples
- Contrarian angles
- Potential hooks
- Knowledge gaps

## Research Depth Levels

### Quick (~5 min)
- 2-3 targeted searches
- Extract top stats
- One contrarian angle
- Surface-level examples
- Good for: familiar topics, quick posts

### Standard (~15 min)
- 5-7 searches across categories
- Verify stats with sources
- Multiple perspectives
- 2-3 solid examples
- Vault connection search
- Good for: most content

### Deep (~30+ min)
- Comprehensive multi-source research
- Primary source hunting
- Expert opinion aggregation
- Competitive content analysis
- Historical context
- Full vault integration
- Good for: cornerstone content, thought leadership

## LinkedIn-Specific Research Focus

For LinkedIn content, prioritize:

### Stat Hunting
- Industry-specific numbers
- Trend data (year-over-year changes)
- Surprising percentages
- Before/after comparisons

### Example Finding
- Recognizable company stories
- Relatable professional experiences
- Failure-to-success narratives
- Counter-intuitive outcomes

### Angle Discovery
- What's everyone saying? (So you can say something different)
- What's been debated recently?
- What's the unpopular truth?

## Quality Checks

Before finalizing research:
- [ ] Stats are from 2024-2025 (or explicitly noted as older)
- [ ] Sources are credible (not just blogs)
- [ ] At least one contrarian angle found
- [ ] At least one concrete example
- [ ] Personal connection identified
- [ ] Multiple hook options available
