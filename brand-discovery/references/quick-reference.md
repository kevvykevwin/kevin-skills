# Brand Discovery Quick Reference

Cheat sheet for live discovery calls.

---

## Story Questions (Pick 3-5)

| Question | What You're Learning |
|----------|---------------------|
| "What problem pissed you off enough to start this?" | Origin energy, authenticity |
| "One sentence a customer would use to describe you?" | Positioning clarity |
| "What are you absolutely NOT?" | Anti-positioning, boundaries |
| "What makes you different — really different?" | Differentiation depth |
| "Brand you admire (not a competitor)?" | Aspirational direction |
| "Words that should NEVER describe you?" | Voice guardrails |

---

## Visual Questions (Pick 3-5)

| Question | What You're Learning |
|----------|---------------------|
| "Walk me through your logo. Love it, tolerate it, hate it?" | Attachment level |
| "Are your colors documented with hex codes?" | Documentation maturity |
| "When was your site last updated? Who built it?" | Freshness, ownership |
| "Does Instagram match your homepage?" | Consistency reality check |
| "Do you have any style guide? Even messy?" | Governance level |
| "Who approves creative — one person or everyone wings it?" | Brand ownership |

## Framing Lines

**Opening the topic**:
> "Before we get into campaign strategy, I want to understand your brand foundation — because even the best ads underperform if the creative doesn't feel cohesive."

**Surfacing the upsell**:
> "There's an opportunity here to build on what's working and create consistency. Right now, there might be a gap between your ads and your site that's leaving money on the table."

**If they're hesitant**:
> "Totally get it — not always the right time. We can work within constraints for now, but I'd rather be upfront: we may hit a ceiling on performance until this gets addressed."

**Introducing designer**:
> "If you don't already have someone for this, we work with designers who specialize in this kind of foundation work. Either way, we'll stay involved to make sure it translates into better campaigns."
