# Pre-Call Research Protocol

Detailed guide for gathering brand context before discovery calls.

## Search Strategy

### 1. Company Website Deep Dive

**Pages to check**:
- Homepage (hero messaging, visual first impression)
- About / Our Story (origin narrative, values, team)
- Product/Services pages (how they describe what they do)
- Blog (voice, topics they care about)
- Press/News section (what they want to highlight)
- Footer (taglines, mission statements often hidden here)

**Capture**:
- Exact tagline/mission statement if present
- Key phrases they use repeatedly
- Visual screenshots for audit

### 2. Founder/Leadership Research

**Search queries**:
- "[Founder name] interview"
- "[Founder name] podcast"
- "[Company name] founder story"
- "[Founder name] LinkedIn"

**Looking for**:
- Origin story in their own words
- How they describe the problem they're solving
- Personal background that informs brand POV
- Media training level (polished vs. authentic)

### 3. Press & Media Coverage

**Search queries**:
- "[Company name] press"
- "[Company name] news"
- "[Company name] announcement"
- "[Company name] featured in"
- site:techcrunch.com "[Company name]"
- site:forbes.com "[Company name]"

**Looking for**:
- How journalists describe them (external perception)
- Key milestones (funding, launches, partnerships)
- Quotes from leadership
- Coverage tone (hype-y, critical, neutral)

### 4. Social Media Audit

**Platforms to check**:
- Instagram (visual consistency, content style)
- LinkedIn (professional positioning)
- TikTok (if B2C — are they even trying?)
- Facebook (often neglected, tells you a lot)
- Twitter/X (voice, engagement style)

**Capture**:
- Follower counts (relative benchmarks)
- Posting frequency
- Visual consistency across platforms
- Engagement quality
- Screenshot grid for visual audit

### 5. Competitor Visual Comparison

**Process**:
1. Identify 2-3 direct competitors (ask client or research)
2. Screenshot competitor homepages
3. Note: logo style, color palette, photography style, typography feel
4. Identify where client fits or stands out

**Questions to generate**:
- "Are you intentionally different from [Competitor] visually, or did it just happen?"
- "I noticed [Competitor] uses [visual approach]. Is that something you'd want to emulate or avoid?"

### 6. Ad Library Research (If Applicable)

**Meta Ad Library**: https://www.facebook.com/ads/library
**Google Ads Transparency Center**: https://adstransparency.google.com

**Looking for**:
- What creative are they currently running?
- Visual consistency with brand
- Messaging themes
- Quality level

## Red Flags to Note

During research, flag these indicators of deeper brand issues:

- **Website copyright date is 2+ years old**: Site hasn't been touched
- **Different logos across platforms**: No brand governance
- **About page is generic or missing**: No story defined
- **Stock photos everywhere**: No visual identity investment
- **Inconsistent color usage**: No style guide in practice
- **Founder has strong voice but brand doesn't**: Disconnect between leader and company
- **Recent rebrand that looks worse**: Someone made a bad call
- **No social presence at all**: Either intentional or neglected (find out which)
