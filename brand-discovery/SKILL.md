# Brand Discovery Diagnostic

Surface brand gaps and creative opportunities during client discovery. Produces designer briefs and investment recommendations.

## Two-Track Diagnostic

### Track 1: PR & Story (The Narrative)
What do they *stand for*? What's written vs. unwritten?

### Track 2: Visual Identity (The Execution)
What do they *look like*? Is it cohesive or chaos?

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Call Research

Before the discovery call, gather context. Use web search to find:

### Business Context
- Company website (especially About, Our Story, Team pages)
- LinkedIn company page + founder profiles
- Press coverage, interviews, podcast appearances
- Crunchbase/funding history (if relevant)
- Social media presence (Instagram, TikTok — visual consistency)

### Competitive Landscape
- 2-3 direct competitors for visual comparison
- Industry visual standards and expectations

### Existing Brand Signals
- Current tagline, mission statement, values (if published)
- Visual consistency across touchpoints (site vs. social vs. ads)
- Quality of existing creative (ads library if available)

**Output**: 1-page research brief with key observations and questions to probe.

---

## Phase 2: Discovery Conversation

### Track 1: PR & Story Questions

**Origin & Identity**
1. "What's the founding story? What problem pissed you off enough to start this?"
2. "If a customer had to describe you in one sentence to a friend, what would you want them to say?"
3. "What are you absolutely *not*? Who's your anti-customer?"

**Positioning & Differentiation**
4. "Who do you see as your main competitors? What makes you different — really different, not just 'better quality'?"
5. "What's the one thing you want people to remember after interacting with your brand?"
6. "Is there a belief or point of view you hold that your competitors would disagree with?"

**Voice & Personality**
7. "If your brand were a person at a party, how would they act? What would they talk about?"
8. "Show me a brand you admire — not a competitor, just a brand whose *vibe* you'd want to borrow."
9. "What words should never be used to describe you?"

**Existing Assets**
10. "Do you have a tagline, mission statement, or brand manifesto written down anywhere?"
11. "Any press coverage or interviews we should know about?"
12. "What's worked before? What messaging has resonated?"

### Track 2: Visual Identity Questions

**Logo & Mark**
1. "Walk me through your logo. Who made it? When? Do you love it, tolerate it, or hate it?"
2. "Do you have different versions (horizontal, stacked, icon-only)? Vector files?"

**Color System**
3. "What are your brand colors? Are they documented with hex codes, or is it 'roughly that blue'?"
4. "Are colors used consistently across your site, social, packaging, ads?"

**Typography**
5. "What fonts do you use? Are they licensed, or did someone pick them years ago and now nobody knows?"
6. "Is there a primary/secondary font system, or just vibes?"

**Photography & Imagery**
7. "What's your photography style — lifestyle, product-focused, user-generated? Custom or stock?"
8. "Do you have a library of approved images, or does everyone just grab what they need?"

**Website & Digital**
9. "When was your site last updated? Who built it — agency, freelancer, in-house, DIY?"
10. "Does your site reflect who you are *now*, or who you were 3 years ago?"

**Consistency Audit**
11. "Show me your Instagram. Now show me your homepage. Do they feel like the same brand?"
12. "If I looked at your Google ads and your email templates side-by-side, would they match?"

**Existing Documentation**
13. "Do you have any brand guidelines or style guide? Even a messy one?"
14. "Who's the keeper of the brand? Is there one person who approves creative, or does everyone just wing it?"

## Phase 4: Investment Tier Assessment

Based on audit findings, categorize the work needed:

### Tier 1: Quick Polish — $2,500–5,000
**Situation**: Assets exist, just need organization and documentation.

**Signals**:
- Logo is solid, but no vector files or variations
- Colors are used consistently but not documented
- Photography style exists but isn't codified
- "We have everything, it's just scattered"

**Deliverable**: Brand style guide documenting existing assets + minor refinements

**Timeline**: 2-3 weeks

---

### Tier 2: Foundation Work — $5,000–15,000
**Situation**: Gaps in the visual system need filling. Core assets may need refresh.

**Signals**:
- Logo is dated or inconsistent usage
- Color palette is muddy or inconsistent
- No typography system
- Photography is random stock/UGC mix
- Site and social feel like different brands
- "We've been meaning to fix this for a while"

**Deliverable**: Refreshed core assets + comprehensive style guide + templates

**Timeline**: 4-6 weeks

---

### Tier 3: Strategic Rebrand — $15,000–30,000+
**Situation**: Fundamental misalignment between who they are and how they look.

**Signals**:
- Logo no longer reflects the business
- Visual identity built for different era/market
- Company has evolved but brand hasn't
- Multiple acquisitions/pivots = Frankenstein brand
- "We hate everything about how we look"

**Deliverable**: Full brand identity system — strategy, logo, color, type, photography, guidelines

**Timeline**: 8-12 weeks

## Conversation Framing

When presenting findings to the client:

### Lead with Opportunity, Not Problems

**Instead of**: "Your brand is a mess and it's hurting your ads."

**Try**: "You've got solid bones here. The opportunity is to build on what's working and create consistency across touchpoints. Right now, someone might see your ad, click through, and feel like they landed on a different company's site — that gap is leaving money on the table."

### Connect to Performance

"We can run the best campaigns in the world, but if the creative doesn't feel cohesive, conversions will suffer. Think of brand work as infrastructure — it compounds across everything we do."

### Position the Upsell Naturally

"This is something we can either work with a designer on together, or if you don't have someone already, we work with [designer name/agency] who does this kind of foundation work. Either way, we'll be involved to make sure it translates into better campaign performance."

### If They Push Back on Investment

"Totally get it — this isn't always the right time. What we can do is document what exists and work within those constraints for now. But I'd rather be upfront: we may hit a ceiling on performance until this gets addressed."
