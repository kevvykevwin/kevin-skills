# Fractional CMO Frameworks

Strategic frameworks for C-suite marketing leadership, focused on translating business goals into actionable marketing plans with measurable outcomes.

## Core Workflow

### Step 1: Discovery & Diagnosis
Understand the business context before building strategy.

**Client Context Questions:**
- Current audience: Who's buying now? What channels are active?
- Metrics tracked: What ties to revenue/leads? What's measured today?
- Budget landscape: Current spend? Triggers for increased investment?
- Historical performance: What worked? Why? Seasonality patterns?
- Revenue trends: Growth trajectory? Inflection points?

**Data to Request:**
- Revenue trending (12-24 months)
- Current marketing metrics and reporting
- Any market research (see references/audience-research-methodology.md)
- Media performance history
- Seasonality indicators

**Output:** Business context brief documenting goals, constraints, and opportunities.

### Step 2: Quarterly Planning
Build structured plans that connect strategy to execution.

**Planning Structure:**
1. **Goals** - What we're achieving this quarter (revenue, awareness, expansion)
2. **Roadmap** - Strategic phases and key milestones
3. **Audiences** - Who we're targeting and why
4. **Timeline** - Monthly checkpoints and deliverables

See references/quarterly-plan-template.md for detailed structure.

**Prioritization Framework:**
- Align initiatives to primary business objective
- Sequence based on dependencies (awareness before conversion)
- Build in testing phases for new channels/audiences
- Plan for measurement windows

**Output:** Quarterly plan document with goals, roadmap, audience strategy, and timeline.

### Step 3: Vendor & Media Planning
Evaluate channels and partners with scenario modeling.

**When Evaluating Vendors:**
- What problem does this solve?
- How does it fit the customer journey?
- What's the measurement approach?
- What are the budget scenarios?

**Scenario Modeling:**
For each channel, model Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive scenarios based on:
- CPM ranges for target audience
- Expected impression → visit rate
- Conversion rate assumptions
- Projected revenue impact

See references/media-planning-scenarios.md for calculation examples.

**Output:** Channel recommendations with projected performance across scenarios.

### Step 4: Metrics & Measurement
Define what success looks like and how to measure it.

**Core Metrics Framework:**
- **Revenue** - Ultimate outcome
- **ROAS** - Return on ad spend
- **Spend** - Budget utilization
- **Funnel Metrics** - Page view → Add to cart → Checkout → Purchase

**Measurement Timing:**
- Pre-campaign baseline
- During-campaign monitoring (ideally daily)
- Post-campaign lift analysis

**Goal:** Isolate revenue attribution and measure lift from marketing efforts.

See references/metrics-benchmarks.md for industry standards.

### Step 5: Business Diagnostics
Determine if problems are marketing-driven or operations-driven.

**Diagnostic Framework:**

**If seeing business softness:**

1. **Check Marketing Funnel Performance:**
   - Traffic levels vs baseline
   - Conversion rates by stage
   - Channel-specific performance

2. **Check Operational Funnel:**
   - Inventory availability
   - Checkout friction/abandonment
   - Fulfillment times
   - Customer service issues

3. **Find the Gap:**
   - High traffic, low conversion = Operations problem
   - Low traffic, normal conversion = Marketing problem
   - High add-to-cart, low purchase = Checkout/pricing problem
   - High brand lift, low purchases = Product-market fit question

**Common Patterns:**
- "Tons of add to carts, no purchases" → Checkout friction or pricing
- "Huge brand lift, people aren't buying" → Product/offer mismatch
- "Traffic down, conversion normal" → Awareness/acquisition issue
- "Everything down" → Broader market or competitive pressure

See references/diagnostic-checklist.md for detailed decision tree.

**Output:** Clear diagnosis of whether issue is marketing, operations, or both, with recommended actions.

## Best Practices

**For C-Suite Communication:**
- Lead with business outcomes, not marketing tactics
- Use scenario modeling to show range of possibilities
- Tie every recommendation to revenue/growth metrics
- Be clear about what you can vs cannot influence

**For Planning:**
- Start with business goals, work backward to tactics
- Build in testing and learning phases
- Plan measurement from the start
- Leave room for optimization based on results

**For Diagnostics:**
- Look at the full customer journey, not just marketing metrics
- Compare performance to baseline, not just absolute numbers
- Consider external factors (seasonality, competition, economy)
- Separate correlation from causation
