# Quarterly Planning Template

Structure for creating actionable quarterly marketing plans that connect strategy to execution.

## Document Structure

### Cover / Vision
Start with the strategic context:
- Quarter and year
- Overarching theme or focus
- Vision statement or guiding principle

**Example:** "Performance marketing is picking the fruit. Brand marketing is watering the tree."

## Section 1: Goals
Define what success looks like this quarter.

**Framework:**
- **Primary Objective** - The one thing that matters most (revenue target, market expansion, brand awareness)
- **Supporting Objectives** - 2-3 goals that enable the primary
- **Success Metrics** - How we'll know we achieved it

**Example Goals:**
- Drive $X in revenue from new customer acquisition
- Expand into 3 new geographic markets
- Achieve Y% brand awareness lift in target segment

## Section 2: Audience Clusters
Define who you're targeting with clear segmentation.

**For Each Cluster:**
- **Name** - Memorable persona label
- **Core Identity** - "Style & Impact", "Family & Values", "Wellness & Adventure"
- **Pain Points** - What frustrates them (3-4 bullets)
- **Opportunities** - How to win them (one clear statement)
- **Authentic Quote** - In their voice

**Example:**
```
Grounded Adventurers + Rooted Wellness Seekers
"Products that match how intentionally I live the rest of my life."

Pain points:
- "I can't tell who's actually real anymore."
- "I've been burned by 'clean' claims before."

Opportunity: Be the brand they don't have to vet.
```

**Keep it to 2-4 clusters maximum** to maintain focus.

## Section 3: Roadmap
Strategic phases showing progression through the quarter.

**5-Phase Framework:**
1. **Awareness Channels** - Narrow down channels that drive lift
2. **Audiences** - Finding new complementary + auxiliary audiences
3. **Storytelling** - Creative that works on 'mute', new prevailing threads
4. **Channel Expansion** - New social channels (TikTok, Pinterest, Substack)
5. **Expansion** - Testing additional markets, geo strategy

**For Each Phase:**
- Key activities
- Expected outcomes
- Dependencies
- Success criteria

**Visual:** Create a horizontal timeline showing phases and milestones.

## Section 4: Channel Partners
Detailed plans for each media channel.

### Channel Template:
**Channel Name** (e.g., "Disney+/Hulu", "Spotify", "YouTube")

**Timeline:** Visual calendar showing deployment windows

**Scenarios:** Model 3 budget scenarios
| Scenario | CPM | Impressions | Visits | Purchases | Projected Revenue |
|----------|-----|-------------|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Conservative | $X | XXX,XXX | XXX | XX | $X,XXX |
| Moderate | $X | XXX,XXX | XXX | XX | $X,XXX |
| Aggressive | $X | XXX,XXX | XXX | XX | $X,XXX |

**Assumptions:** Document the assumptions behind projections
- CPM based on cohort targeting
- Imp-to-visit rate (%)
- Conversion rate (%)
- AOV (average order value)

**Strategic Note:** Why this channel? What's unique about it?

**Example:**
"YouTube is doing heavy lifting because of the 'clickable' format"
"This is non-skippable content" (for Hulu)
"Endorsement effect doesn't show up as easily for 'imp-to-visit' rates" (for podcasts)

### Repeat for Each Channel:
- Spotify (Audio vs Video focus)
- Podcast Networks (Host-read vs Programmatic)
- YouTube (Skippable vs Non-skippable)
- Social platforms
- Display/Programmatic
- Email/CRM

## Section 5: Timeline
Month-by-month execution plan.

**Monthly Structure:**
```
January:
- Video partners deployment
- [Specific activation 1]
- [Specific activation 2]

February:
- Early signals, adjust mix
- New audience attributes + testing
- [Specific activation]

March:
- New video creative
- [Specific activation]
- End-of-quarter measurement
```

**Include:**
- Partner onboarding dates
- Creative deadlines
- Testing windows
- Optimization checkpoints
- Measurement milestones

## Key Checkpoints Framework
Essential milestones that should appear in every plan:

1. **Content Creation**
   - Content calendar for the year (promos, always-on, narrative opportunities)
   - Style guides and mood boards
   - Shoot dates for new footage

2. **Media Deployment**
   - Build media plan based on content calendar and audience insights
   - Channel activation dates
   - Budget allocation by channel

3. **Testing & Learning**
   - New audience segments to test
   - Creative variations
   - Channel experiments
   - Geo expansion tests

4. **Measurement**
   - Establish brand measurement (YouGov, Qualtrics, etc.)
   - Attribution setup
   - Lift studies
   - Incrementality testing

## Best Practices

**Keep it Visual:**
- Use timeline graphics
- Show phase progression
- Include data tables for scenarios
- Add charts for audience composition

**Make it Actionable:**
- Every section should have clear next steps
- Assign owners where appropriate
- Include deadlines
- Link to detailed briefs

**Stay Strategic:**
- Connect tactics back to goals
- Explain the "why" behind choices
- Show scenario thinking
- Build in flexibility for optimization

**Document Assumptions:**
- What benchmarks are you using?
- What historical data informs projections?
- What needs to be true for success?
- What could cause plan to change?

## Review Cadence

**Weekly:** Progress against timeline
**Monthly:** Performance vs projections, optimization decisions
**Quarterly:** Full plan review, next quarter planning kickoff
