# Metrics & Benchmarks

Industry benchmarks and measurement frameworks for tracking marketing performance across different business models.

## Core Metrics Framework

### Primary Metrics
Track these for every client, every campaign:

**Revenue** - Ultimate outcome
- Total revenue attributed to marketing
- Revenue by channel
- Revenue by audience segment
- New vs repeat customer revenue

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** - Efficiency measure
```
ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
```
- Overall ROAS
- Channel-specific ROAS
- Campaign ROAS
- Audience segment ROAS

**Spend** - Budget utilization
- Total spend vs budget
- Spend by channel
- Spend pacing (daily/weekly/monthly)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)

**Funnel Metrics** - Conversion pathway
- Page views
- Add to cart (ATC)
- Checkout initiated
- Purchase completed

### Secondary Metrics
Context-dependent tracking:

**Brand Metrics:**
- Brand awareness (aided/unaided)
- Brand consideration
- Purchase intent
- Net promoter score (NPS)

**Engagement Metrics:**
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Engagement rate
- Time on site
- Pages per session

**Audience Metrics:**
- New vs returning visitors
- Customer lifetime value (LTV)
- Repeat purchase rate
- Cohort retention

## Industry Benchmarks by Business Model

### D2C E-commerce

**Conversion Rates:**
- Site-wide: 2-4%
- Email: 3-6%
- Paid social: 1-3%
- Paid search: 3-5%
- Organic social: 0.5-1.5%

**ROAS Targets:**
- Blended: 3-5x
- Meta/Facebook: 3-6x
- Google Search: 4-8x
- Google Display: 2-4x
- YouTube: 2-4x
- Pinterest: 2.5-4x

**Funnel Metrics:**
- PV → ATC: 8-12%
- ATC → Checkout: 50-65%
- Checkout → Purchase: 70-85%

**AOV (Average Order Value):**
- Fashion: $50-150
- Beauty: $40-80
- Home goods: $75-200
- Supplements: $45-90

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
- Target: < 1/3 of LTV
- Sustainable: < 30% of AOV
- Efficiency threshold: Payback < 6 months

### B2B SaaS

**Conversion Rates:**
- Visitor → MQL: 2-5%
- MQL → SQL: 10-30%
- SQL → Opportunity: 20-40%
- Opportunity → Close: 20-30%

**Cost Metrics:**
- Cost per MQL: $50-200
- Cost per SQL: $200-800
- Customer Acquisition Cost: $1,000-10,000+
- CAC payback period: 12-18 months

**ROAS / ROI:**
- Paid search: 3-5x
- Paid social: 2-4x
- Content marketing: 5-8x (longer attribution window)
- Events/Conferences: 4-6x

**Funnel Metrics:**
- Demo request rate: 1-3% of visitors
- Trial signup: 2-5% of visitors
- Trial → Paid: 10-25%

### B2B Services/Consulting

**Lead Generation:**
- Cost per lead: $75-400
- Lead → Qualified: 20-40%
- Qualified → Meeting: 30-50%
- Meeting → Proposal: 50-70%
- Proposal → Close: 20-40%

**Channel Performance:**
- LinkedIn: CPL $100-300
- Google Search: CPL $75-250
- Content/SEO: CPL $50-150
- Referral: CPL $0-50

**Conversion Timeline:**
- First touch → Close: 30-90 days (SMB)
- First touch → Close: 90-180 days (Mid-market)
- First touch → Close: 180-365+ days (Enterprise)

### Retail/Omnichannel

**Digital Metrics:**
- E-commerce conversion: 2-4%
- Click-and-collect: 4-6%
- Store locator → Visit: 15-25%

**Attribution:**
- ROPO (Research Online, Purchase Offline): 40-60%
- In-store influenced by digital: 30-50%

**Unified ROAS:**
- Digital + In-store lift: 5-8x
- Pure digital: 2-4x

## Measurement Framework

### Pre-Campaign Baseline
Establish before launching any initiative:

**Historical Performance (4-12 weeks):**
- Daily/weekly revenue trend
- Channel performance
- Conversion rates by stage
- Seasonality patterns

**Baseline Metrics:**
- Average daily visits
- Average daily revenue
- Standard funnel conversion rates
- Brand search volume

### During-Campaign Monitoring

**Daily (Performance Campaigns):**
- Spend pacing vs budget
- ROAS tracking
- Volume metrics (impressions, clicks, visits)
- Early conversion signals

**Weekly (All Campaigns):**
- Funnel performance vs baseline
- Channel performance trends
- Creative performance
- Audience segment performance
- Budget utilization

**Bi-weekly:**
- Optimization decisions
- Creative refresh needs
- Budget reallocation
- Forecast updates

### Post-Campaign Analysis

**Immediate (Within 48 hours):**
- Final spend vs budget
- Final ROAS
- Volume achieved vs target
- Direct attribution revenue

**7-Day Post:**
- Extended attribution window
- View-through conversions
- Brand search lift
- Site traffic decay curve

**30-Day Post:**
- Full attribution
- Incrementality analysis
- Brand lift measurement
- Cohort analysis of acquired customers

## Diagnostic Calculations

### Efficiency Metrics

**Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):**
```
CPA = Total Spend ÷ Number of Acquisitions
```

**Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):**
```
LTV = (Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Average Customer Lifespan)
```

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
```
Target: 3:1 or higher
Healthy: > 3:1
Concerning: < 2:1
```

**Payback Period:**
```
Months to Payback = CAC ÷ (Monthly Revenue per Customer - Monthly Variable Costs)
```

### Funnel Analysis

**Conversion Rate by Stage:**
```
CR = (Stage Completions ÷ Previous Stage) × 100
```

**Drop-off Analysis:**
```
Drop-off % = 100 - Conversion Rate
```

**Funnel Efficiency:**
```
Overall Conversion = (Purchases ÷ Initial Visits) × 100
```

### Channel Performance

**Channel-Specific ROAS:**
```
ROAS = Revenue from Channel ÷ Spend on Channel
```

**Blended ROAS:**
```
Blended ROAS = Total Revenue ÷ Total Spend
```

**Incremental ROAS:**
```
Incremental ROAS = (Revenue with Campaign - Baseline Revenue) ÷ Campaign Spend
```

## Red Flags & Triggers

**Performance Issues:**
- ROAS drops >20% week-over-week → Investigate immediately
- CPA increases >30% → Check competition, creative fatigue
- Conversion rate drops >15% → Check site issues, pricing, inventory

**Opportunity Signals:**
- ROAS consistently >target by 50%+ → Increase budget
- Audience segment performing 2x+ others → Shift budget allocation
- New channel testing >target ROAS → Scale up

**Market Signals:**
- Brand search increasing → Awareness working
- Direct traffic increasing → Brand equity building
- Organic social engagement up → Content resonating

## Benchmarking Best Practices

**Compare Appropriately:**
- Your performance vs your historical baseline (most important)
- Your performance vs industry benchmarks (context)
- Your channels vs each other (optimization)

**Avoid:**
- Comparing to top 10% performers without understanding context
- Ignoring seasonality in comparisons
- Comparing different business models directly

**Focus On:**
- Trends over time
- Relative performance (channel A vs B)
- Improvement rates
- Efficiency gains
