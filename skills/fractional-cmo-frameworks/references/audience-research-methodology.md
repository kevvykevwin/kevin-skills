# Audience Research Methodology

Framework for conducting and presenting audience research to inform targeting and creative strategy.

## Research Tooling

**Primary Tools:**
- **YouGov** - Consumer intelligence with 24M+ panel members, updated biweekly
- **Explorium** - AI-driven data platform for correlating attributes and go-to-market insights
- Platform native analytics (Meta, Google, etc.)
- First-party customer data

## Research Framework

### 1. Define Audience Segments
Start with hypothesis-driven segments:
- Current customer proxy (baseline)
- Aspir

ational segments (who you want to reach)
- Adjacent markets (logical expansion)

**Example from Maggie's Organics:**
- Current Customer Proxy (11.5M est. market)
- Eco-Conscious Fashion (55.8M est. market)
- Outdoor Enthusiasts (57.7M est. market)

### 2. Demographics Analysis
For each segment, document:
- Age distribution (with index vs general population)
- Race/ethnicity composition
- Gender split
- Education levels
- Employment status
- Marital status
- Geographic concentration (urban/suburban/rural)

**Key Questions:**
- Where do segments over-index?
- What's the overlap between segments?
- Are there clear geographic hotspots?

### 3. Income & Spending Power
- Household income distribution
- Index against general population
- Identify sweet spot income brackets
- Consider regional COL differences

**Highlight the concentration** - e.g., "Cohort is on the higher end $100-200k"

### 4. Geography (DMA Analysis)
Map audience concentration by metro area:
- Which DMAs over-index?
- Coastal vs interior distribution?
- Large metro vs mid-size cities?

**Strategic Implication:** "Smaller, mid-sized cities may be more optimal for top-of-funnel awareness plays"

### 5. Psychographics
Capture attitudes, values, behaviors:

**Love, Leisure, Lifestyle:**
- Diversity of experiences
- Travel preferences
- Adventure seeking
- Environmental consciousness
- Work-life balance

**Values & Purchasing:**
- Sustainability preferences
- Handmade vs mass-produced
- Brand authenticity
- Social responsibility

**Free Time Activities:**
- Top hobbies and interests
- Media consumption habits
- Community involvement
- Health & wellness activities

### 6. Media Consumption
Two dimensions to analyze:

**Media Footprint** (Receptivity to Advertising Index + Heavy Usage Index):
Plot channels on axes to find sweet spots - high receptivity + high usage

**Media Consumption** (Light/Medium/Heavy by channel):
- Catch-up TV
- Live TV
- Streaming (YouTube, Hulu, Netflix)
- Social platforms (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
- Audio (Spotify, Podcasts, Radio)
- Traditional (Print, OOH)

**Identify overlaps** - "There is a large overlap for exercise + helping out the community" suggests partnership opportunities

### 7. Advertising Attitudes
Measure receptivity to:
- Email marketing
- Social media ads
- Influencer content
- Brand activism
- Sponsorships
- Direct mail

**Key insight from Maggie's:** "They appreciate brands who have a say in social issues"

## Presentation Structure

### Executive Summary
- Total addressable market size for each segment
- Key demographic differences
- Recommended priority segments

### Segment Profiles
For each audience:
1. Size & composition
2. Demographics snapshot
3. Psychographic highlights
4. Media consumption patterns
5. Advertising receptivity

### Audience Clusters
Synthesize into actionable clusters with:
- **Persona name** - "Grounded Adventurers", "Clean Life Moms", etc.
- **Core identity** - One-sentence essence
- **Pain points** - What frustrates them
- **Opportunities** - How to win them
- **Quote** - Authentic voice

**Example:**
```
"Cool Guys" & Conscious Connectors
"I want fewer, better things I can actually talk about."
Pain point: "Ethical brands either can't tell their story or make me feel guilty."
Opportunity: Be the cool thing he discovered, not the thing he was sold.
```

### Strategic Recommendations
- Priority audiences to target
- Channel recommendations by segment
- Creative implications
- Testing roadmap

## Key Checkpoints
Before finalizing research:
- [ ] Audience segments clearly defined with size estimates
- [ ] Demographics indexed against general population
- [ ] Psychographics reveal authentic motivations
- [ ] Media consumption shows clear channel priorities
- [ ] Clusters synthesized with pain points and opportunities
- [ ] Strategic recommendations tie to business goals
- [ ] Creative implications documented
