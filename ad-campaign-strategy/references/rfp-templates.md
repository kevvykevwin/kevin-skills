# RFP Templates

Templated Request for Proposal documents and outreach emails for engaging advertising vendors. Designed for quick customization using find-replace.

## Template Structure

All templates use **[BRACKETS]** for easy find-replace:
- [CLIENT_NAME]
- [BUSINESS_DESCRIPTION]
- [BUDGET]
- [FLIGHT_START]
- [FLIGHT_END]
- [AUDIENCE_TARGET]
- [TIMELINE_RESPONSE]
- [YOUR_NAME]
- [YOUR_EMAIL]
- [YOUR_PHONE]

## Standard RFP Template

### Subject Line:
```
RFP: [CLIENT_NAME] - [CHANNEL] Advertising Partnership
```

### Email Body:

```
Hello [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

I'm reaching out on behalf of [CLIENT_NAME] to explore a potential advertising partnership with [VENDOR_NAME].

ABOUT [CLIENT_NAME]:
[BUSINESS_DESCRIPTION]

CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES:
[Primary objective - e.g., "Drive qualified traffic and conversions for Q1 2025"]

BUDGET & FLIGHT:
- Total Budget: [BUDGET]
- Flight Dates: [FLIGHT_START] - [FLIGHT_END]
- Billing: [NET_30 / NET_60 / etc.]

TARGET AUDIENCE:
[AUDIENCE_TARGET]

TARGETING REQUIREMENTS:
[Specific targeting capabilities needed - e.g., "Geographic: US only, Demographics: Ages 25-54, Interests: Sustainable living, outdoor activities"]

DELIVERABLES REQUESTED:
- Media plan with recommended tactics
- CPM/CPC pricing
- Projected reach and frequency
- Reporting capabilities and frequency
- Creative specifications
- Attribution methodology

TIMELINE:
- RFP Response Due: [TIMELINE_RESPONSE]
- Decision: [DECISION_DATE]
- Campaign Launch: [FLIGHT_START]

Please provide:
1. Recommended media plan and rationale
2. Pricing (CPMs, fees, minimums)
3. Audience targeting capabilities
4. Case studies (if available in similar category)
5. Reporting samples
6. Creative specifications
7. Timeline for implementation

I'm happy to schedule a call to discuss further. Please let me know your availability.

Best regards,
[YOUR_NAME]
[YOUR_TITLE]
[YOUR_EMAIL]
[YOUR_PHONE]
```

## Follow-Up Email Templates

### Initial Non-Response (5-7 days)

```
Subject: Re: RFP: [CLIENT_NAME] - [CHANNEL] Advertising Partnership

Hi [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

I wanted to follow up on the RFP I sent last week regarding [CLIENT_NAME]'s upcoming campaign.

I understand you're likely busy, but we're on a tight timeline and would love to include [VENDOR_NAME] in our consideration.

Is this something your team can support? If you need any additional information, I'm happy to provide it.

Our response deadline is [TIMELINE_RESPONSE]. Please let me know if that's feasible.

Thanks,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

### Second Follow-Up (If still no response)

```
Subject: Final Follow-Up: [CLIENT_NAME] RFP

Hi [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

This is my final follow-up regarding the [CLIENT_NAME] RFP.

We're moving forward with partner selection by [DATE] and I wanted to give you one last opportunity to participate.

If I don't hear back by [FINAL_DEADLINE], I'll assume [VENDOR_NAME] is unable to support this campaign and will proceed with other partners.

Please let me know either way so I can plan accordingly.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

### Response Received - Schedule Call

```
Subject: Re: [CLIENT_NAME] RFP - Next Steps

Hi [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

Thank you for the proposal! I've reviewed it and have a few questions. Would you have time for a brief call to discuss?

I'm available:
- [DATE/TIME OPTIONS]

Looking forward to discussing the partnership in more detail.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Negotiation Email Templates

### Request for Better Pricing

```
Subject: Re: [CLIENT_NAME] Proposal - Pricing Discussion

Hi [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

Thank you for the detailed proposal. The plan looks strong, but we're working within a tight budget.

Our target CPM is [TARGET_CPM] based on similar campaigns. Is there flexibility to get closer to this rate, especially if we commit to [BUDGET_COMMITMENT] or [LONGER_TERM / MULTI-QUARTER] partnership?

Also exploring:
- Reducing managed service fees
- Volume discounts
- Performance incentives

Open to creative solutions that work for both sides. Let me know what's possible.

Thanks,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

### Request for Value-Adds

```
Subject: Re: [CLIENT_NAME] Partnership - Additional Value Discussion

Hi [VENDOR_CONTACT_NAME],

We're excited about the proposal and close to moving forward. Before finalizing, I wanted to explore potential value-adds that could make this partnership even stronger:

- Complimentary brand lift study
- Creative production support
- Extended attribution window
- Additional reporting/insights
- Beta access to new features
- [Other relevant asks]

What might be available to include in the package?

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Best Practices for RFPs

### Before Sending:

**Prepare:**
- [ ] Clear understanding of client objectives
- [ ] Budget approved and confirmed
- [ ] Audience research completed
- [ ] Flight dates finalized
- [ ] Decision-making timeline set
- [ ] Evaluation criteria defined

**Vendor Research:**
- [ ] Confirm vendor fit for objective
- [ ] Find correct contact (not generic inquiry@)
- [ ] Review vendor capabilities vs needs
- [ ] Check if existing relationship exists

### RFP Content:

**Always Include:**
- Clear business description
- Specific objectives (not just "increase sales")
- Realistic budget ranges
- Defined audience (not just "everyone")
- Timeline with milestones
- Decision criteria
- Contact information

**Never Include:**
- Unrealistic expectations ("10x ROAS guaranteed")
- Vague budgets ("depends on performance")
- Overly restrictive requirements
- Confidential competitive information
- Multiple conflicting objectives

### After Sending:

**Follow Up:**
- Day 5-7: First follow-up if no response
- Day 10-12: Second follow-up
- Day 14: Final deadline communication

**Response Management:**
- Create comparison spreadsheet
- Schedule calls within 48 hours of receiving proposal
- Ask clarifying questions
- Request case studies/references
- Negotiate thoughtfully

### Evaluation Criteria:

**Score Vendors On:**
1. Strategic fit (25%)
2. Pricing/value (25%)
3. Targeting capabilities (20%)
4. Reporting/attribution (15%)
5. Account management (10%)
6. Creative support (5%)

**Document Decision Rationale:**
- Why vendor was selected
- What alternatives were considered
- Key negotiation points
- Lessons for future RFPs

## RFP Checklist

Before hitting send:

- [ ] Find-replace all [BRACKETED] placeholders
- [ ] Spell-check
- [ ] Correct vendor/contact name
- [ ] Realistic timeline (allow 1-2 weeks for response)
- [ ] Appropriate tone (professional but approachable)
- [ ] Clear next steps
- [ ] Contact information included
- [ ] Attachments if needed (creative brief, brand guidelines)
- [ ] CC'd appropriate team members

After sending:

- [ ] Set reminder for follow-up
- [ ] Add to CRM/tracking system
- [ ] Create comparison spreadsheet
- [ ] Calendar time for proposal reviews
- [ ] Prepare evaluation criteria
