## Vendor Evaluation Framework

### Initial Vetting

**Required Information:**
- [ ] Targeting capabilities relevant to our client
- [ ] Minimum spend requirements
- [ ] Attribution/reporting capabilities
- [ ] Creative specifications
- [ ] Account management structure
- [ ] Case studies in similar categories
- [ ] Payment terms

### Performance Tracking

**Track for Each Vendor:**
- CPM/CPC vs benchmarks
- CTR
- Conversion rate
- ROAS
- Quality of traffic (bounce rate, time on site)
- Reporting quality
- Account management responsiveness

### Quarterly Review Process

**Every quarter, evaluate:**
1. **Performance Ranking:** Sort vendors by ROAS
2. **Volume Analysis:** Are we spending enough to matter?
3. **Quality Assessment:** Traffic quality, fraud rates
4. **Innovation:** New capabilities, beta opportunities
5. **Relationship:** Account management quality

**Decision Framework:**
- **Top 25%:** Increase investment, expand
- **Middle 50%:** Maintain, optimize
- **Bottom 25%:** Review for potential removal
- **New vendors:** Test with 10-15% of budget

### Remove When:
- Consistent underperformance (3+ months)
- Fraud/quality issues
- Poor account management
- Minimum spends no longer aligned
- Better alternatives identified

## Budget Allocation Across Vendors

### Portfolio Approach:

**Proven Performers (60% of budget):**
- Top 3-5 vendors with consistent ROAS
- Scale what works
- Optimize for efficiency

**Testing (30% of budget):**
- Established vendors with new targeting/formats
- Different creative approaches
- Audience expansion

**New Vendors (10% of budget):**
- Pilot programs
- Emerging platforms
- Strategic bets

### Diversification Rules:

**Don't:**
- Put >40% of budget with single vendor (risk)
- Spread too thin (<$2k/month per vendor = can't learn)
- Test more than 2-3 new vendors per quarter (management burden)

**Do:**
- Maintain 3-5 core vendors
- Test 1-2 new vendors per quarter
- Review and reallocate quarterly
- Document learnings for future

## Partner Negotiation

### Negotiable Items:

**Always negotiate:**
- CPM rates (especially at volume)
- Managed service fees
- Payment terms (Net 30 vs Net 60)
- Reporting frequency/access
- Creative production support

**Sometimes negotiable:**
- Minimum spends (if good fit/high spend elsewhere)
- Contract length
- Termination clauses
- Performance guarantees (rare but possible)

**Negotiation Leverage:**
- Higher budget commitments
- Longer-term contracts
- Multiple clients/accounts
- Category exclusivity

### Red Flags:

- Unrealistic performance promises ("10x ROAS guaranteed")
- No minimum spend transparency
- Complex fee structures
- Lock-in contracts without performance clauses
- Resistance to sharing case studies or references
- Poor tech/platform demos
